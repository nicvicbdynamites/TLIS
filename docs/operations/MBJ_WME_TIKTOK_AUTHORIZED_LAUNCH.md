## 1. Scope-of-authority check (before launch)

Record outside this public repository the signed agreement(s), approved representatives, expiration, and permitted activities. Do not upload confidential authorization documents or account credentials to this repository.

- **Talent approval:** Does the agreement authorize use of Michael B. Jordan's name, likeness, and the term "official" for this proposed TikTok account? Document approved scripts and signatory.
- **Agency approval:** Separately confirm if WME authorizes its name, logo, account identity, recruiting-related messaging and/or agency spokesperson claims. Permission from one party does **not** imply the other's approval.
- **Industry access:** Do not offer direct talent access, talent-agent meetings, casting callbacks, agency representation or fees without explicit approval and a legitimate fulfillment process.
- **Approved links:** Use only verified, owner-approved public-facing contact, press and application destinations.
- **Brand kit and music:** Check usage rights for all video clips, stills, music and trademarks.
- **Synthetic media:** Obtain any required performer/voice consent and apply TikTok's realistic-AI disclosure rules.

### Owner-controlled account

1. Existing official account? Prefer an explicitly approved owner-controlled account over making an unofficial duplicate.
2. If a new channel is authorized, the talent/agency owning party registers it with an organization-controlled email, enables passkeys/2FA and maintains recovery ownership.
3. Owner assigns IWD access through TikTok Business Center partner/member asset permissions where available. Avoid sharing login secrets or OTPs.
4. Select the approved handle, name, verified identity and business/creator configuration. Availability and verification are determined by TikTok.
5. Owners approve the final account bio and contact path. Proposed bio for approved joint account only: **"Michael B. Jordan • Films, culture & community. Managed by authorized team."**
6. If WME is *not* an authorized brand owner for this channel, remove WME from public account identity; WME may only be mentioned in sourced, accurately contextualized posts.

## 4. Approval checklist for every video

1. Producer logs rights-cleared source files, hook, script, visual treatment and audio license.
2. Creative lead checks quality, caption, correct facts, subtitles, community guidelines and AI labels.
3. Talent-designated approver confirms use of identity, messaging and likeness. Record approver identifier/time **in the authorized operational system**, not this public repository.
4. Agency-designated approver separately checks WME-specific wording, trademarks, representation and opportunity links where applicable.
5. Publisher verifies correct destination TikTok account and final rendered version; producer schedules only **after** approvals.
6. Native TikTok publishing: account owner or authorized operator posts in TikTok, then records the exact full public video URL in TLIS.
7. Monitor replies; route press/agency/talent inquiries to approved official contact paths, without promising personal replies or soliciting fees.

## 5. TLIS wiring / status boundaries

**Current architecture (inspected October 11, 2026):**
- TLIS `/accounts` stores TikTok account metadata in the `tiktok_accounts` Supabase table. This does NOT register a TikTok account or grant OAuth authorization.
- The connected Supabase project named `TLIS Intelligence System` was reported INACTIVE during this setup. Re-activation must be separately approved and verified.
- The Review Center's campaign records are browser-local. This PR fixes the misleading publish button to require approved status + an externally published TikTok URL, and changes scheduling language to calendar-only.
- Recording a URL is **manual evidence, not independent API verification**. TLIS must not claim it created or verified the TikTok post.
